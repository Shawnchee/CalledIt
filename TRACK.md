# Track B — Consumer & Fan Experiences (TxLINE World Cup Hackathon)

> **Status:** product = **CalledIt** (concept locked, MVP in build). Repo: `Shawnchee/CalledIt`.
> See [`README.md`](./README.md) for the product. This file is the hackathon brief, kept as reference.

## The brief
Most fans watch the World Cup with a phone in their hand. TxLINE exposes live scores, real-time
odds, and match events for all 104 games — data that until now only big operators could access.
Build a **consumer fan experience** on top of it. Scope is allowed to be deliberately small;
clarity of use case + quality of execution beats scope.

## What judges score
- **Fan Accessibility & UX** — would a mainstream, non-technical fan regularly open this?
- **Real-Time Responsiveness** — does it update fluidly as the match unfolds?
- **Originality & Value Creation** — genuinely new fan interaction, not a repackaged feed.
- **Commercial & Monetization Path** — is there a viable product/business angle?
- **Completeness & Execution** — feels like a finished end-to-end feature, even if small.

## Hard constraints
- Must use **TxLINE data as a live input**.
- Must **sign up through Solana** (wallet auth).
- Must be a **functional deployed product** (devnet/mainnet ok), not a mockup.
- **Heavily judged on the demo video** (≤5 min) — matches end after deadline, so the video must
  carry the product experience.

## Submission checklist
- [ ] Demo video (≤5 min, Loom/YouTube) — problem + live walkthrough + how TxLINE powers it

## Seed ideas from the brief (not committed)
- Group Sweepstake — friends assigned teams, leaderboard updates live from TxLINE.
- AI Pundit Bot — Telegram bot narrating goals/red cards/odds shifts (bonus: TTS).
- Hi-Lo Stats Game — guess if next stat (shots/corners/possession) is higher/lower, build a streak.
